TripSplit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

What we collect

  • Google account basics: your name, email address and profile photo, used only to sign you in and show you to your tripmates.
  • Trip data you enter: trip names, dates, expenses, amounts, categories and expense locations.
  • Payment SMS (optional): if you turn on UPI payment alerts, the app reads payment SMS on your phone to suggest expenses. An expense is only saved when you add it.

How we use it

Only to run the app: signing in, syncing trips between members, and calculating balances. Trip data is visible to the members of that trip.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with third parties.

Deleting your data

Email sb.sapientinfo@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and its data.
